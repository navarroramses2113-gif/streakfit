Questions

Good to know

Can I use Forja now?

Yes. The beta runs in your phone's browser today. On iPhone, add it to your Home Screen for reminders. The App Store app comes next, and your streak carries over.

Is it free?

Yes. Counting, streaks, friends, the Feed and leagues are free. Forja Pro adds extras, and Founding Members get it free for a year.

Does Forja record me?

No. The video is checked on your phone and never saved or uploaded. Our server only sees a list of body-point positions for each set, with no images.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to tell you when Forja launches and about your founder price. Nothing else, and we never sell it. Email hello@getforja.app and we'll delete it.

Do I need equipment?

No. Just your phone and a bit of floor. Walks and runs count too.

Android?

The beta works in any modern phone browser, Android included.
